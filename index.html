Quick rulesHidden teams: only you know your card, and you learn others' teams by Glancing at them or when they reveal their card. Glance to learn a player's team. Each turn move your marker 1–3 wedges clockwise and do that action. Attack (mandatory if any pawn is on your space; a neutral pawn dies on one hit): roll the die (faces 2–6 and a sword) — the sword always hits, a number hits if it beats the pawns on the space. Losing ♥ lets you reveal your role for its ability (King: free rondel action, Queen: up to 3 waltzes, Knight: spin pawns out of your space, Bard: pull pawns into your space — pick one variant below). Lose 3 ♥ and you're out; a dead King takes their whole team. After the first King falls, all outer edge spaces close to Waltz. Last team standing wins. Neutral roles and the Emperor pawn are not in this version.
